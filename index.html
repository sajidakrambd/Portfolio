About Sajid Akram

Sajid Akram — Graphic Designer

I'm Sajid Akram, a brand identity and graphic designer with 7+ years of experience creating logos, visual identity systems, packaging, advertising and social media design for 50+ clients in Bangladesh and the UAE. Working across creative agencies and the printing industry means my designs look right on screen and print right at the press.

I start with the business, not the logo: who the customer is, what the competition looks like and what the brand needs to be remembered for. Then I turn that thinking into one consistent system that a team can apply without guesswork. AI-assisted tools help me explore ideas faster; every final decision and every final file is crafted by hand and production-ready.

Questions

Branding & Design FAQ

What does a branding designer do?

A branding designer creates the visual side of a business's identity: the logo, colours, typography, imagery style and the rules for using them. The aim is to make the business recognisable, credible and consistent on packaging, social media, print and web.

How much does brand identity design cost?

Cost depends on scope. A logo alone is smaller than a full identity with packaging, social media templates and brand guidelines. Send a short brief and I will reply with a quote and a clear list of deliverables.

What is included in a branding project?

Typically a discovery brief, logo concepts and a final logo suite, colour palette, typography, brand mock-ups and usage guidelines. Packaging, social media templates, stationery and company profiles can be added as needed.

How long does a branding project take?

A logo or a focused identity usually takes two to four weeks. A full system with packaging or social templates takes longer. The biggest factor is how quickly feedback comes back between rounds.

Why is visual identity important?

People judge a business in seconds. A consistent visual identity builds recognition and trust, makes marketing more efficient and helps a business look as good as the service it delivers.

How do you work with clients internationally?

I have delivered work for clients in Bangladesh and the UAE and collaborate remotely by email and WhatsApp. Scope, milestones and feedback rounds are agreed at the start, and final files are delivered digitally in print-ready and web-ready formats.

Do you design packaging and print-ready files?

Yes. I spent 2.5 years designing and running print production in Dubai, so packaging comes with dielines and colour-managed, print-ready artwork that a printer can use straight away.

Are you available for freelance projects?

Yes. I take freelance branding, packaging and social media design projects alongside my role at M/S Digiden in Dhaka. Email or WhatsApp a short brief to get started.
