About Me

Sajid Akram — Graphic Designer

I am a Graphic Designer with 7+ years of professional experience in branding, social media, advertising, print design, digital marketing, and print production. My experience across both creative agencies and the printing industry has given me a strong understanding of designing impactful visuals that are both aesthetically compelling and technically production‑ready.

By combining creativity, strategic thinking, and AI‑assisted creative tools, I create innovative, user‑focused solutions while continuously adapting to the latest design trends and technologies. Originally from Chattogram, now based in Dhaka — with 2.5 years of hands‑on experience designing and operating print production in Dubai, UAE.
